About ToolBench AI Directory

ToolBench AI Directory is a gallery of ready-to-use AI prompts for tattoo, vector, and illustration ideas — fine line, minimalist, vector art, Aztec, floral, and more.

Each entry pairs a written prompt with a reference photo chosen to convey the mood and style the prompt is going for. The reference photos are real photographs used for inspiration — they are not literal outputs of the paired prompt, and results from running the prompt yourself in an AI image tool will vary.

How to use this site

Browse or search for a style, open a design to read the full prompt, copy it, and paste it into an AI image tool such as Midjourney, DALL·E, or Stable Diffusion. Treat each prompt as a starting point — small tweaks to wording, aspect ratio, or model version will change the result.

Submissions

Have a prompt worth adding to the directory? Use the "Submit Design" button on the homepage, or reach out through the contact page.
